# 🛠️ Troubleshooting & FAQ

Found a bug or having issues? Here are the most common solutions.

## 📱 WhatsApp Connection Issues
**Problem**: The status is "DISCONNECTED" or the QR code doesn't load.
- **Solution 1**: Ensure your phone has a stable internet connection.
- **Solution 2**: Log out of all other "WhatsApp Web" sessions.
- **Solution 3**: Restart the connection from the **Settings > WhatsApp** page.

## 💰 Payment Verification Failures
**Problem**: A customer sent a receipt, but the Payment Shield didn't validate it.
- **Solution 1**: Ensure the screenshot is clear and shows the Transaction ID.
- **Solution 2**: Check if the recipient number on the receipt matches your configured payment numbers.
- **Solution 3**: Perform a **Manual Validation** by clicking the "$" icon on the order.

## 🤖 AI is giving wrong information
**Problem**: The AI is quoting wrong delivery fees or old prices.
- **Solution**: Go to **Settings > Knowledge Base**. Ensure your delivery zones and FAQ are up to date. The AI uses this data as its "Bible".

## 🚀 App feels slow
- **Solution**: Vendeur IA is a PWA. Try clearing your browser cache or re-installing the app from your home screen for the best performance.

## 📞 Still need help?
Use the **Copilot Widget** in the dashboard to chat with our support AI, or use the "Contact Founders" option for critical issues.
