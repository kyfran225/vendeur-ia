# 📦 Catalog & Inventory Management

Managing your products efficiently is the key to high conversion rates. Vendeur IA provides tools to make this effortless.

## 📸 AI Vision (Photo-to-Product)
Instead of typing every detail, simply take a photo of your product.
- **Single Item**: The AI focuses on details, fabric, and style.
- **Batch Scanning**: Take a photo of a shelf or a collection. The AI will identify multiple items and create draft entries for all of them.

### Tips for Better Results:
- Use good lighting.
- Place the product against a neutral background.
- Include price tags in the photo if possible; the AI will read them!

## ✍️ Smart Copywriting (Captions)
Every product has a "Sparkle" button. Click it to generate:
- **Instagram/TikTok Captions**: Optimized for engagement with relevant hashtags.
- **Local Tone**: Choose between a professional, friendly, or "Nouchi" (Ivorian street style) vibe.

## ⭐ Featured Items
Select your best-sellers to appear in the **Hero Showcase** on your public storefront. These items get 3x more visibility.

## 📉 Stock Tracking
- Mark items as "Out of Stock" to prevent the AI from suggesting them.
- The AI will automatically alert you when a popular item is running low.
