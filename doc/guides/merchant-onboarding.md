# 🚀 Merchant Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Vendeur IA! This guide will help you set up your digital shop and get your AI Sales Agent running in minutes.

## Step 1: Account Creation
1. Connect via **Google Auth** on the landing page.
2. Complete the **Magic Onboarding** wizard:
    - Choose your business category (Fashion, Food, Service, etc.).
    - Define your delivery zones and fees.
    - Set up your Mobile Money numbers (Wave, Orange, MTN).

## Step 2: WhatsApp Integration
1. Go to **Settings > WhatsApp**.
2. Scan the **QR Code** using your WhatsApp mobile app (Linked Devices).
3. Wait for the status to turn green (**CONNECTED**).

## Step 3: Populate your Catalog
- Use **AI Vision**: Take photos of your products. The AI will automatically extract names, descriptions, and prices.
- Review and publish your items to the **Public Shop**.

## Step 4: Test your IA
1. Open the **Playground** or send a message to your shop's WhatsApp number.
2. Observe how the IA greets customers, suggests products, and handles orders.
3. If needed, adjust the **Tone of Voice** in AI Settings.

## Step 5: Start Selling
- Share your **Public Shop URL** (e.g., `vendeur.ia/shop/your-store`) on Instagram and TikTok.
- Monitor your **Money Board** for real-time sales performance.
