# 🌟 Features Catalog - Vendeur IA OS

Vendeur IA is packed with features designed to automate the entire sales cycle for social commerce entrepreneurs.

## 1. 🚀 Magic Onboarding
- **Instant Demo**: Test the AI Sales Agent on a simulated WhatsApp interface immediately.
- **Business Wizard**: Configure category (Fashion, Food, Service), location, and currency in < 1 minute.
- **Spotlight Tour**: Interactive guided tour for first-time users.

## 2. 🧠 AI Sales Agent (The Ghost Seller)
- **24/7 Availability**: Responds to customers instantly at any time.
- **Multilingual Support**: Masters French, Nouchi (Ivory Coast), and Wolof (Senegal) tones.
- **Multi-Channel**: Syncs WhatsApp, Instagram Marketplace, and TikTok messages.
- **Human Takeover**: Instant toggle for the merchant to resume manual control.

## 3. 📸 AI Vision & Catalog
- **Photo-to-Product**: Take a photo, and the IA generates the full product sheet.
- **Batch Scanning**: Upload multiple items at once; the IA categorizes them all.
- **Smart Captions**: One-click generation of viral TikTok/Instagram descriptions.
- **Featured Items**: Interactive "Hero Showcase" for the public storefront.

## 4. 💰 Money Board & Payments
- **Sales Pipeline**: Real-time tracking from Discovery to Delivery.
- **Payment Shield**: Automated verification of Mobile Money screenshots (Wave, Orange, MTN).
- **Digital Receipts**: Instant generation and delivery of receipts via WhatsApp.
- **AI Growth Stats**: Analytics showing how much time and money the IA saved the merchant.

## 5. 📦 Order & Logistics
- **Order Manager**: Full lifecycle tracking of every sale.
- **Delivery Dispatch**: One-click sharing of delivery details to couriers via WhatsApp.
- **Accounting Export**: Export sales data to CSV for bookkeeping.

## 🛍️ Public Shop (Storefront)
- **Mobile-First Vitrine**: A high-end shopping experience for customers.
- **AI Search**: Voice-controlled product search.
- **Smart Cart**: Multi-product checkout with automated delivery fee calculation.
